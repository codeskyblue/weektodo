## Description

WeekTodo is an offline weekly task planner built around the week. Focus on this week, review the last one and plan the next one.

Features:

• Week views: see 1, 2 or 3 weeks side by side and move between weeks quickly
• Tasks: subtasks, dates, complete, drop and drag to reorder
• Categories: separate lists for work, personal and anything else
• Weekly report: generate a summary of the week, with optional AI polish
• WebDAV sync: sync with the desktop app or other devices
• Appearance: light, dark or system, in Chinese and English
• Offline first: data stays on your device, no account required

AI polish is optional and uses your own OpenAI-compatible endpoint and API key. WebDAV sync uses your own server. The developer does not collect your task data.
