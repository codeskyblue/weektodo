WeekTodo 支持

数据保存在哪里？

任务和设置保存在设备本地。卸载 App 会删除本地数据，建议定期使用设置里的备份/导出功能。

如何和桌面版同步？

在设置 → WebDAV 同步中填写你的 WebDAV 地址、用户名和密码，然后点击“立即同步”。桌面版使用同一个 WebDAV 目录即可。

AI 润色需要付费吗？

AI 润色是可选功能，需要你自己提供 OpenAI 兼容接口和 API Key。相关费用由你的接口服务商收取，WeekTodo 不收取额外费用。

周报数据会上传吗？

除你主动配置的 AI 接口或 WebDAV 服务器外，周报和任务数据不会离开设备。

如何反馈问题？

请通过 GitHub Issues 提交问题，并附上 iOS 版本、App 版本和复现步骤。

WeekTodo Support

Where is my data stored?

Tasks and settings are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes local data, so use the backup/export option in Settings regularly.

How do I sync with the desktop app?

Open Settings → WebDAV sync, enter your WebDAV URL, username and password, then tap Sync now. Use the same WebDAV folder on the desktop app.

Does AI polish cost anything?

AI polish is optional and requires your own OpenAI-compatible endpoint and API key. Your provider bills you directly; WeekTodo does not charge extra.

Is my weekly report uploaded?

Reports and tasks never leave your device unless you explicitly configure an AI endpoint or WebDAV server.

How do I report a problem?

Open a GitHub Issue with your iOS version, app version and steps to reproduce.
